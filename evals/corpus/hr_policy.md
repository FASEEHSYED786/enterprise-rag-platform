# HR Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Unused leave of up to five days may be carried over into the next year.

## Sick leave
Employees receive full pay for the first ten days of sickness absence in any rolling twelve months. A fit note is required after seven consecutive days.

## Remote work
Staff may work remotely up to three days per week with line manager approval.
